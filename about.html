  • Who even is this guy?

    As stated all over this website, my name is Alex Baidanuta, and I'm a girl. Suprise! That seems to catch a lot of people off-gaurd who hear my name before seeing my face.

    I'm an illustrator with a deep love for storytelling and character work, which largely comes from being a fan girl throughout my life. I've loved drawing since I was a child, and knew I wanted to pursue it as a career ever since I discovered it was even an option. Some of the times I'm happiest are when I get to illustrate.

    I believe entertainment is one of the most powerful ways we connect with one another. Through film, music, comics, video games, and live performances, people have the opportunity to share their ideas, creativity, and the worlds they imagine with others. It's about taking something that once existed only in someone's mind and bringing it to life in a way that others can experience. It's what allows us to fall in love with fictional characters, see pieces of ourselves reflected in art, lose ourselves in unfamiliar worlds, and find joy, comfort, and inspiration in someone else's work. To me, there's something incredibly special about making things that leave an impression on people, and that's what continues to drive my passion for art and storytelling.
